# Part III: Fundamental Rights

## Article 21: Protection of Life and Personal Liberty

No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to
procedure established by law. Article 21 is considered the heart of the
Constitution and has been interpreted expansively by the Supreme Court.

## Article 14: Right to Equality

The State shall not deny to any person equality before the law or the equal
protection of the laws within the territory of India. Article 14 references
Article 21 in matters of personal liberty.

## Article 32: Right to Constitutional Remedies

The right to move the Supreme Court by appropriate proceedings for the
enforcement of fundamental rights is guaranteed. Article 32 establishes the
Supreme Court as the guardian of fundamental rights.
