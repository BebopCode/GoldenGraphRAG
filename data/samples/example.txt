Ada Lovelace was an English mathematician, born in London in 1815. She is best
known for her work on Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine. Babbage was a
polymath and inventor who designed the Analytical Engine in the 1830s. Lovelace
described an algorithm for the Analytical Engine that is often called the first
computer program. The Analytical Engine was a mechanical general-purpose
computer that was never fully built during Babbage's lifetime. London is the
capital of the United Kingdom.
